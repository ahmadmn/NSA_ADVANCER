Advance table (degrees at each RPM point)

Each slot is the advance at that RPM; the board interpolates linearly between neighbouring points and holds the last value above 7000 RPM. Orange outline = edited, not yet sent. “Send changes” updates the running table; “Save to flash” keeps it after power-off. Range is 0–15° in 1° steps. The two highlighted slots are the ones currently being interpolated. The dashed line is the board's table, the solid line is your edit.
